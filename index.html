Málaga Slow is the personal project of Marcos and Jose, two lifelong friends united by a shared passion: our city.

We are convinced that the best way to discover Malaga is not from a bus window, but through the eyes of those of us who grew up in its streets. We want to show you the soul of our land from a different perspective, combining the comfort of our electric vehicles with the closeness and professionalism you deserve. From the most iconic corners to those secrets that only locals know, we are here so that you don't just see the city, but so that you feel it and understand it just as we live it.
