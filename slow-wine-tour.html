Slow Wine Tour

Experience the ultimate fusion of culture and flavor in this exclusive journey. Your adventure begins with a 60-minute private Tuk-tuk tour, where you will explore the city's most emblematic landmarks. We will cruise through the vibrant Port and Muelle Uno, past the colorful Centre Pompidou and the historic La Farola lighthouse, continuing along La Malagueta beach and the elegant El Limonar district, even passing by the home of the legendary Pablo Picasso. A strategic stop at the Gibralfaro Castle Viewpoint will provide the ultimate panoramic views to understand the city's rich heritage.

The experience continues at a premium seaside wine bar, where you will enjoy a guided tasting of 6 local Málaga wines. This gastronomic immersion is paired with a curated selection of Iberian cured meats, artisanal cheeses, and a professional tasting of two premium extra virgin olive oils.

120 min 70 € / pax

Water included

Stay hydrated while you explore. We provide a fresh bottle of mineral water for every guest on our private Málaga tour.

Capacity

Our Tuk Tuks carry up to 4 passengers, making this the perfect private experience for families or small groups.

Safety

Our tours are safe, secure, and suitable for children from 4 years old, ensuring a worry-free experience in the historic center.
